Fintech · Multi-agent research · Proposal

Silk River: risk first, evidence on demand.

A strong multi-agent risk engine sat under a flat interface where every report carried equal weight. I rebuilt the information architecture around risk itself: one recommendation and confidence score on top, collaborating agents' findings underneath.
